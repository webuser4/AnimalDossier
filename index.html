American Flamingo

A large pink wading bird found in parts of the Caribbean, Mexico, and northern South America. It uses its specialized bill to filter-feed on small organisms and often lives in large colonies.

Cownose Ray

A large, flat-bodied ray found in warm coastal waters of the western Atlantic. It has wing-like pectoral fins, a distinctive head shape, and feeds mainly on clams, oysters, and other bottom-dwelling animals.

Galapagos Tortoise

A giant tortoise native to the Galápagos Islands. It has a large shell, strong legs, and a long lifespan, and mainly eats grasses, leaves, fruits, and other vegetation.

LEARN MORE

Sumatran Orangutan

A large, reddish-brown ape found only on the Indonesian island of Sumatra. It spends most of its time in trees and mainly eats fruits, leaves, and other plants.
